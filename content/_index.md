I am an AI engineer on the ==Digital Transformation== team at [Hanwha Ocean](https://www.hanwhaocean.com/), where I build applied AI/ML systems. I studied Mechanical Engineering at [Yonsei University](https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/), where my research centered on ==autonomous driving== and ==reinforcement learning==. My recent work, **UTMR**, tackles high-speed uncertainty in world-model-based trajectory selection for end-to-end driving, presented at the Autoware Tutorial & Workshop at IEEE IV 2026. I also contributed to work on ==multi-agent reinforcement learning== and ==self-taught reasoning==, including [FlickerFusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15876) (ICLR 2025) and [AdaSTaR](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2505.16322) (NeurIPS 2025). I'm broadly interested in ==agentic systems== and how they can make autonomous decision-making more robust under uncertainty.
